# Legal drafts

These five drafts describe the checked-in implementation and proposed community rules. They are not final policies. The app labels them as requiring owner and legal review.

Publication requires confirmed operator identity, contact information, eligibility rules, privacy legal bases, service-provider disclosures, international transfers, retention rules, privacy-rights processing, refund criteria, moderation and appeal procedures, account-deletion processing, and legal jurisdiction. No facts in those areas have been invented.

Support requests are stored through authenticated Supabase RPCs. No operator delivery, response SLA, refund issuance, or automatic account deletion is implemented by this feature. The deletion action records a review request only.
